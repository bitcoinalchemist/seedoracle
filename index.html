    Hexagrams

    The same figure has a binary value (0–63 in decimal) and a King Wen number (1–64). This is a lookup between two orderings; the lines do not change. Fu Xi orders these figures by binary value; King Wen gives each a traditional number. Bits are read from the bottom line upwards.
